⏰ Why this runs daily Security vulnerabilities are published publicly the moment they are discovered. That means attackers know about a weakness the same day it is announced and immediately start scanning for unpatched apps. A daily scan keeps your exposure window to 24 hours or less. Weekly means up to 7 days of risk. Monthly means up to 30. Daily is the right call for a live app with real users.
